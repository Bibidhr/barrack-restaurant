Our Setting

Six floors above Gairidhara, a world apart

Perched atop the Everest Bank Building in the diplomatic corridor of Gairidhara and Naxal, Copacabana fuses Latin American culinary warmth with breezy open-air rooftop dining.

Whether you arrive for a leisurely afternoon lunch, sunset cocktails as golden light bathes the western hills, or an intimate dinner under the Kathmandu stars, every corner offers thoughtful hospitality.

I. 360° Panorama Unobstructed valley & Himalayan hill vistas
II. All-Weather Deck Covered glass lounge & open breeze terrace
III. Daily 11–11 Uninterrupted service 7 days a week
